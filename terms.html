Risk warning

A crawl is a recreational activity with real risks. You'll be moving through busy streets, crossing roads, using stairs, lifts and rooftops, often at night, in crowds and after drinking. Alcohol slows your reactions and affects your judgement. Falls, trips, traffic and other injuries can happen. You take part at your own risk and you're responsible for your own safety and choices.

Walk, don't run

The clock is part of the game, not a reason to rush. Never run, jaywalk or take risks to beat it. If time runs out while you're heading to a bar, you still have 10 minutes of last orders to get there and it still counts. Stop before you look at the app, and keep your eyes on the road when crossing.

Rooftop Roulette is about visiting bars, not how much you drink. Nothing in the game needs you to drink alcohol: water, a soft drink or nothing at all is fine. Know your limits, venues can refuse service and staff have the final say. Never drive after drinking.
