Who it's for

You need to be 18 or over, and at least the legal drinking age where you're using it.

Drink responsibly

Rooftop Roulette is about visiting rooftops, not how much you drink. Nothing in the game needs you to drink alcohol. Order water, a soft drink or nothing at all. Venues can refuse service, and staff have the final say. Never drive after drinking.

Getting around

You're responsible for how you get between bars and for your own safety. Watch the road, not the app, when you're crossing streets.

Bar information

We work to keep opening hours and details up to date, but venues change things without notice. A bar might be closed, full, or have a dress code or entry fee. Check before you rely on it, and use “Wrong hours or info? Let us know” on a bar's card to tell us when something's wrong. Venues in the app aren't affiliated with or endorsing Rooftop Roulette.
