Delete your Rooftop Roulette account

How to delete your account and the data that goes with it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rooftop Roulette and go to My Tab.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top, or go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

It happens straight away.

By email

Can't get into the app? Email hello@rooftoproulette.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What's deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and profile photo.
  • Your tab's backup: crawls (bars, times, routes, stats, names), the photos on them, and your Hit list, Favourites, Pass list and bars you've been to.
  • Your list of crawl mates and any invites waiting for you.

What's kept

  • Crawls on your phone stay there until you delete the app. They're stored on your device, not with us.
  • Live crawls you hosted or followed stay with the other people on them, as copies on their tabs.
  • Anonymous usage stats aren't linked to your account, so there's nothing to tie back to you.

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion.
