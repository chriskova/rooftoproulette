Support

Stuck? Most answers are here. If not, email hello@rooftoproulette.com.

How do check-ins work?

When you get within about 75 metres of the bar you spun, your visit is logged automatically. Stay 3 minutes and it counts.

My visit didn't log

  • Make sure location is allowed: Settings → Permissions.
  • Keep the app open on the way. If your phone was locked, open the app when you arrive and it'll catch up.
  • GPS can be patchy between tall buildings. Step outside or near a window for a moment.

A bar's details are wrong

Open the bar's card and tap “Wrong hours or info? Let us know”. Tell us what's changed (hours, closed, moved) and we'll fix it for everyone.

I'm not in Sydney or Brisbane

Try the demo crawl to see how it works, or explore another city's rooftops to plan a trip. More cities are coming.

Where are my clips?

Open a crawl in My Tab and tap its photo, or use ⋯ → Photos & clips. In the app, clips can save straight to your photos (Settings → Save clips to gallery). See how much space they use, or clear them, in Settings → Clips on this phone.

Moving to a new phone

There are no accounts yet, so your tab lives on your phone. Use Settings → Back up My Tab to save a file, then Restore My Tab on the new phone.

Something else broken?

Settings → Report a problem opens an email with your app version and device already filled in. Tell us what happened and what you expected.
