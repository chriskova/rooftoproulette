Rooftop Roulette

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Short version: your crawls live on your phone. If you choose to sign in, your tab (crawls, crawl photos and lists) is backed up to your account so you don't lose it. Live crawls share the bars and times with the people you invite. We don't sell data and we don't run ads. We collect anonymous usage stats to make the app better, and you can turn that off in Settings.

What stays on your phone

Your crawls are stored on your device: the bars you visited and when, your route, your stats, your Hit list, Favourites and Pass list, crawl names, the photos you add to a crawl card and the clips you film. Clips never leave your phone unless you share them. If you're not signed in, none of it leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

Your account (optional)

You can play without an account. If you sign in, with Google or with an email and password:

  • What we store: your name, email address and, for Google sign-in, your profile photo; a backup of your tab (crawls with their bars, times, route, stats, names and the photos you added to them, plus your Hit list, Favourites, Pass list and the bars you've been to, but not clips); and the people you've been on crawls with, so you can invite them again.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). They're stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read them, including us.
  • Where: Google Firebase. Your crawl backups are stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • Who can see it: you. Other users can't see your backup. It's encrypted in transit and at rest by Google, and access to our database is limited to the Rooftop Roulette team. We only look at an account's data to help with a request you've made or if the law requires it.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and your backup. Crawls on your phone stay there.

Even without an account, the app gets an anonymous sign-in ID from Firebase so it can use live crawls. It isn't linked to your name or email.

Live crawls

When you invite people to follow your crawl, the crawl is sent to our database (Firebase) while it runs: the bars you've been to and where you're heading, the times, your crawl's name, your first name, and the first names of people following. Anyone with the invite link can see it. Your exact GPS location and route aren't shared, only the bars. When it ends, people who followed get a copy on their tab. Invites to mates you've crawled with before appear in their app.

Your location

The app uses your location to find rooftops near you and to check you in when you arrive at a bar. That happens on your phone. Your location isn't sent to us.

Camera and microphone

Only used when you film a spin or take a photo for your crawl card. Clips stay on your phone, and in your photo gallery if you've chosen to save them there. Photos do too, and if you're signed in, the ones on your crawls are also backed up to your account.

Services the app relies on

  • Google Maps Platform shows maps and loads bar photos and details. Google receives those requests, like any map you open. See Google's privacy policy.
  • A walking-route service (OpenStreetMap routing) can be used to draw your route along real streets on your crawl card. It receives the locations of the bars on that route.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) runs sign-in, account backups and live crawls. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • PostHog (hosted in the EU) receives the anonymous usage stats below.

Anonymous usage stats and crash reports

To see what's working, the app sends anonymous events: a random ID made on your phone (not linked to your name or email), the app version, your device type, operating system and screen size, which screens you open, and things like "a 1-hour crawl started" or "a crawl ended with 4 bars after 60 minutes" and the city it was in. If the app crashes, it sends the error message. PostHog sees your IP address when these arrive, which can indicate a rough area. We never send your name, exact location, photos or clips.

Turn this off any time: Settings → Share usage data.

Sharing a crawl

When you share a crawl card or link, the link itself carries that crawl's details (bars, times and route) so your mates can open it. It isn't stored on our servers, but anyone you send it to can see it.

Emailing us

If you contact us or report a problem, we'll see your email address and whatever you include. We only use it to reply.

Age

Rooftop Roulette is for people 18 and over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions: hello@rooftoproulette.com.
