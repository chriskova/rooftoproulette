Rooftop Roulette

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Short version: your crawls live on your phone. If you choose to sign in, your tab (crawls, crawl photos and lists) is backed up to your account so you don't lose it. Live crawls share the bars, times and your crawl zone with the people you invite. We don't sell data and we don't run ads. We collect usage stats that aren't linked to your name or email, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Who we are

Rooftop Roulette is a registered business name in New South Wales, Australia (ABN 42 887 720 837). "We" and "us" in this policy means Rooftop Roulette. Contact us at hello@rooftoproulette.com.

What stays on your phone

Your crawls are stored on your device: the bars you visited and when, your route, your stats, your Hit list, Favourites and Pass list, crawl names, the photos you add to a crawl card and the clips you film. Clips never leave your phone unless you share them. If you're not signed in, none of it is backed up, and deleting the app deletes it.

Your location

The app uses your location to find rooftops near you and to check you in when you arrive at a bar, and that's worked out on your phone. During a crawl, it keeps using your location while the phone is locked or the app is in the background, so arrivals and departures are logged on time. You'll see an "Active crawl" notification (or the location indicator on iPhone) while that's happening, and it stops when the crawl ends. Your live location isn't sent to us. Your location only leaves your phone in these cases:

  • Account backups: if you're signed in, each crawl's route is backed up with it (see below).
  • Live crawls: the bars and the crawl zone you picked, not your GPS position (see below).
  • Crawl links: a crawl you share carries its route inside the link.

Your account (optional)

You can play without an account. If you sign in with Google, with Apple (on iPhone) or with an email and password:

  • What we store: your name, email address and, for Google sign-in, your profile photo; a backup of your tab (crawls with their bars, times, route, stats, names and the photos you added to them, plus your Hit list, Favourites, Pass list and the bars you've been to, but not clips); the people you've been on crawls with, so you can invite them again; and the wildcard packs you've bought. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only get Apple's private relay address.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). They're stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read them, including us.
  • Where: Google Firebase. Your crawl backups are stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • Who can see it: you. Other users can't see your backup. It's encrypted in transit and at rest by Google, and access to our database is limited to the Rooftop Roulette team. We only look at an account's data to help with a request you've made or if the law requires it.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and your backup. Crawls on your phone stay there.

Even without an account, the app gets a random sign-in ID from Firebase so it can use live crawls. It isn't linked to your name or email.

Live crawls

When you invite people to follow your crawl, the crawl is sent to our database (Firebase) while it runs: the bars you've been to and where you're heading, the times, the crawl zone you picked, any wildcards, your crawl's name, your first name, and the first names of people following. Anyone with the invite link can see it. Your exact GPS position and route aren't shared. When it ends, people who followed get a copy on their tab, so the crawl's record stays in our database afterwards. Email us if you want one removed. Invites to people you've crawled with before appear in their app.

Camera and microphone

Only used when you film a spin or take a photo for your crawl card. Clips stay on your phone, and in your photo gallery if you've chosen to save them there. Photos do too, and if you're signed in, the ones on your crawls are also backed up to your account.

Wildcard packs

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat manages purchases for us: it receives a purchase ID (your account ID if you're signed in, otherwise a random one), what you bought and when, and your device type, so your packs unlock and can be restored.

Services the app relies on

  • Google Maps Platform shows maps and loads bar photos and details. Google receives those requests, like any map you open. See Google's privacy policy.
  • A walking-route service (OpenStreetMap routing, run from Germany) can be used to draw your route along real streets on your crawl card. It receives the locations of the bars on that route.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) runs sign-in, account backups and live crawls. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • Apple runs Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases, and Google runs Google sign-in and Google Play purchases.
  • RevenueCat manages wildcard pack purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • PostHog (hosted in the EU) receives the usage stats below.

Usage stats and crash reports

To see what's working, the app sends usage events that aren't linked to your name or email: a random ID made on your phone, the app version, your device type, operating system and screen size, which screens you open, and things like "a 1-hour crawl started" or "a crawl ended with 4 bars after 60 minutes" and the city it was in. If the app crashes, it sends the error message. PostHog sees your IP address when these arrive, which can indicate a rough area. We never send your name, exact location, photos or clips.

Turn this off any time: Settings → Share usage data.

Sharing a crawl

When you share a crawl card or link, the link itself carries that crawl's details (bars, times and route) so the people you send it to can open it. It isn't stored on our servers, but anyone you send it to can see it.

Early access list

If you sign up for early access on rooftoproulette.com, we store your email and which phone you use. It's stored in Google Firebase and only the Rooftop Roulette team can see it. We use it to email you about the launch and to send you early test versions for your phone. Every email has a way to unsubscribe. To be taken off the list, email hello@rooftoproulette.com.

Emailing us

If you contact us or report a problem, we'll see your email address and whatever you include. We only use it to reply.

Data outside Australia

Account backups are stored in Australia. Some of the services above process data overseas: PostHog in the European Union, the walking-route service in Germany, and Google, Apple and RevenueCat in the United States and other countries where they operate. We choose providers that protect data to a high standard.

How long we keep it

Account data and backups stay until you delete your account. Live crawl records stay so the people who followed keep their copy, and we'll remove one if you ask. Early access details stay until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them. Emails stay as long as we need them to deal with your request.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your data

You can see and edit most of your data in the app, and delete your account in Settings. To ask what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, email hello@rooftoproulette.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, email us first and we'll look into it and reply within 30 days. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Age

Rooftop Roulette is for people 18 and over.

Changes

If we make a significant change to this policy, we'll tell you in the app. Smaller changes take effect when we update the date at the top.
