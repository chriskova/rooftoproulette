Rooftop Roulette

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Short version: your crawls live on your phone. We don't have accounts, we don't sell data and we don't run ads. We collect anonymous usage stats to make the app better, and you can turn that off in Settings.

What stays on your phone

Everything about your crawls is stored on your device only: the bars you visited and when, your route, your stats, your Hit list, Favourites and Pass list, crawl names, the photos you add to a crawl card and the clips you film. We never receive any of it. If you delete the app, it's gone, unless you've made a backup file (Settings → Back up My Tab), which you keep wherever you like.

Your location

The app uses your location to find rooftops near you and to check you in when you arrive at a bar. That happens on your phone. Your location isn't sent to us.

Camera and microphone

Only used when you film a spin or take a photo for your crawl card. Clips and photos stay on your phone, and in your photo gallery if you've chosen to save them there.

Services the app relies on

  • Google Maps Platform shows maps and loads bar photos and details. Google receives those requests, like any map you open. See Google's privacy policy.
  • A walking-route service (OpenStreetMap routing) can be used to draw your route along real streets on your crawl card. It receives the locations of the bars on that route.
  • PostHog (hosted in the EU) receives the anonymous usage stats below.

Anonymous usage stats and crash reports

To see what's working, the app sends anonymous events: a random ID made on your phone (not linked to your name or email), the app version, your device type, operating system and screen size, which screens you open, and things like "a 1-hour crawl started" or "a crawl ended with 4 bars after 60 minutes" and the city it was in. If the app crashes, it sends the error message. PostHog sees your IP address when these arrive, which can indicate a rough area. We never send your name, exact location, photos or clips.

Turn this off any time: Settings → Share usage data.

Sharing a crawl

When you share a crawl card or link, the link itself carries that crawl's details (bars, times and route) so your mates can open it. It isn't stored on our servers, but anyone you send it to can see it.

Emailing us

If you contact us or report a problem, we'll see your email address and whatever you include. We only use it to reply.

Age

Rooftop Roulette is for people 18 and over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions: hello@rooftoproulette.com.
